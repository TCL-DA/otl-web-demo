Seven points. One closed loop.

Seven critical temperatures are monitored so you can adjust the roast profile precisely, while the closed loop recycles exhaust air and the integrated afterburner cleans what leaves the system.

  • 1BeanCore bean temperature for precise roasting.
  • 2ExhaustKeeps airflow clean and efficient.
  • 3CoolingRapid cooling that preserves flavour.
  • 4Burner airOptimal heat delivery.
  • 5ChamberConsistent heat in the drum chamber.
  • 6CycloneChaff collection and air circulation.
  • 7AfterburnerFinal stage that cleans exhaust gases.
