Heat you can see

Multiple heating methods in a single roaster — intelligent hot-air flow combined with drum configurations — so every batch matches the last.

Every roast, recorded

Management software records each roast and lets you set a profile for every bean. On the 60 and 120 kg systems, seven temperature points are monitored.

Full circulation

On the industrial models, closed-loop airflow recycles exhaust air and an integrated afterburner cleans emissions, using up to 30% less energy.

Cooling tray

Rapidly cools roasted beans to preserve flavour and prevent over-roasting.
