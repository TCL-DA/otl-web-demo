04 — Technology

Heat, air, data.

Technology is at the heart of our product development: energy efficiency, clean and smart roasting.

01

Multiple heating methods

Intelligent hot-air flow combined with drum configurations in a single roaster, for consistent quality batch to batch.

02

Smart automation

Record, analyse and optimise every roast. Set personal profiles for each roaster’s style.

03

Seven-point monitoring

Bean, exhaust, cooling, burner air, chamber, cyclone and afterburner temperatures on the 60 & 120 kg systems.

04

Clean emissions

Full-circulation airflow and an integrated afterburner, using up to 30% less energy on industrial models.

2025
